# Robot makes a PB& J

## Assumptions

- We have a fully charged robot with empty hands
- Theres a table the following item on it:
    - Jar of PB
    - Knife
    - Jar of Jelly
    - Spoon
    - Loaf of wrapped bread
    - Butter knife.
    - Plate
    - Toaster
    - Napkin

<br>

## Steps to follow
1. Open up the Jars
2. Unpack the Bread, and remove 2 slices
3. Put bread in the toaster for 3 minutes
4. after bread is done cooking, put them on the plate side by side.
5. Pick up the knife, and peanut on the left hand, get a scoup of penut butter, on the knife and spread it on one slice.
6. put down the knife, and peanut butter and grab the spoon, and jelly, scoup some jelly on the spoon, and spread it on the other slice of the bread.
7. Grab the napkin, clean off both knife and spoon, by wiping them both. 
8. put the spoon itself in the sink, while still holding the napkin.
9. put 2 pieces of bread on top of each other, on the sides that have the peanut butter, and jelly sides, facing each others.
10. Put the Napkin in the Trash.
11. Put the lids back on the jars.
12. Put the rest of the bread that is unwraped that didn't have the peanut butter and jelly on it, in the fridge.

## Exit Conditions.
- Constructed sandwich are the only items on the table.
